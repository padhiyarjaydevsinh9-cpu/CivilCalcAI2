📚 Civil Engineering Calculators Online

CivilCalcAI is a free online collection of civil engineering calculation tools. It is designed to make common construction and engineering calculations faster and easier on smartphones and computers.

Concrete Volume Calculator

Concrete volume is calculated using length × width × depth.

Steel Weight Calculator

For round steel bars, the commonly used theoretical unit-weight formula is d² ÷ 162 kg/m, where d is diameter in millimetres.

Brick Calculator

The brick calculator provides an approximate quantity based on wall volume and brick volume. Mortar joints and wastage can change the actual quantity.

RCC Beam Calculator

The RCC beam calculator estimates concrete volume using beam width, depth and length.
