📚 Civil Engineering Calculators Online

CivilCalcAI is a free online collection of civil engineering calculation tools. It is designed to make common construction and engineering calculations faster and easier on smartphones and computers.

Concrete Volume Calculator

The concrete volume calculator uses the basic length × width × depth formula to estimate the volume of concrete required for a rectangular section.

Steel Weight Calculator

For a round steel bar, the commonly used theoretical unit-weight formula is d² ÷ 162, where d is the diameter in millimetres. The calculator multiplies the unit weight by the entered length.

Brick Calculator

The brick calculator estimates bricks from wall area using an approximate number of bricks per square metre. Actual brick quantity can vary with brick dimensions, mortar joints and wastage.

Construction Cost Calculator

Use the construction cost calculator to multiply material quantities by their respective rates and estimate the total entered material cost.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about civil engineering calculations.

What is a concrete volume calculator?
It calculates rectangular concrete volume using length × width × depth. The result is normally expressed in cubic metres when the dimensions are entered in metres.
How is steel weight calculated?
A commonly used theoretical formula for round reinforcement steel is d² ÷ 162 kg/m, where d is the diameter in millimetres.
Is CivilCalcAI free?
